1. Executive Summary

Following the submission of a 17-page proposal titled "Automated Property SEO Acquisition System" by an external contractor requesting ₦1,000,000 setup fees plus ongoing foreign API subscriptions and monthly management retainers, SmartKode Solutions conducted an exhaustive technical audit of the document.

Our engineering audit reveals that the contractor's proposal relies on a generic, third-party template designed for WordPress blogs that fundamentally conflicts with Praise Dynasty's actual custom web architecture. Furthermore, over 70% of the deliverables quoted in their proposal have already been engineered and deployed by SmartKode within Praise Dynasty's existing infrastructure.

2. Critical Flaws in the External Proposal

CRITICAL FLAW #1: Severe Platform Mismatch (The WordPress Blunder) The contractor's entire roadmap (Pages 1, 3, 6, 9, 10, 11, 13, and specifically Milestone 3 billed at ₦200,000) is built around automating updates to a WordPress website.

The Reality: Praise Dynasty does not run on WordPress. It is a custom, high-speed static architecture powered by Tailwind CSS, vanilla JavaScript, Firebase, and Netlify CI/CD. The contractor submitted a ₦1M technical proposal without even inspecting the website's source code. Implementing their workflow would require dismantling Praise Dynasty's fast custom site and downgrading to a slow, vulnerable WordPress platform.
CRITICAL FLAW #2: Charging ₦450,000 for Pre-Existing Systems Milestone 1 (₦450,000) claims to build a "central property database with unique IDs."

The Reality: Praise Dynasty already possesses a fully operational, cloud-synchronized **Firebase Realtime Database** with structured property IDs, pricing, location data, image URLs, and live inventory status, completely managed via your custom admin.html dashboard.
CRITICAL FLAW #3: Hidden Foreign Currency Bills & Zero Guarantee The ₦1,000,000 fee does not cover operational costs. The client is explicitly required to pay ongoing monthly USD subscriptions for scraping tools (DataForSEO) and AI tokens (Section 7), plus a monthly management retainer (Section 8).

Yet, in Section 9 (Page 17), the contractor explicitly disclaims all accountability: "No specific Google ranking, traffic, lead, or sales result is guaranteed."
